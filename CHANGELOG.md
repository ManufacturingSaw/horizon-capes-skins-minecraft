# Changelog


## 1.2.3 — 3 November 2026

A maintenance release.

### Fixes

- Skin preview renders correctly on high-DPI screens.

## 1.2.2 · 2026-11-01

- Cape imports accept uppercase PNG extensions again.
- The skin editor keeps the last tool selected.
- Faster first paint of the preview pane.
- Squad capes keep their colors after a restart.

## 1.2.0 · 2026-10-09

- The wardrobe: pick a piece, see it on your player, press Wear it.
- Squad capes with a join code, and your own cape from a PNG, JPG or GIF.

## 1.2.1 · 2026-10-30

- **Wardrobe**: 24 capes, 8 wings, 12 hats and 6 shoulder pets, all original and all free. Every piece has a preview card; point at one to see it on your player, turn the player with Front and Back or by dragging, then **Wear it**.
- Capes are pixel art in the game's 64 × 32 cape layout, drawn at 4x.
- Your own skin on the player, from Mojang's public profile API.
- **Squad capes**: one cape for your crew from 12 emblems and two colours. Friends join with a code like `WOLF-7Q2K`, and your squad's name shows under your name tag.
- **Make your own**: a cape from any PNG, JPG or GIF up to 512 KB, with a draggable crop. GIFs of up to 32 frames make animated capes.
- **Emotes**: hold B in the game for a wheel of 8 emotes.
- Hide all cosmetics with one switch, and choose whose custom capes you see.
- Founder cape for everyone who connects in the first month.
- Sign-in through the game's own session: no account and no password.
- Portable builds for Windows (`.exe`) and Linux (AppImage).
